The Global Challenge

The Water Paradox in Tropical Rice Farming

Smallholder farmers in South Asia and tropical basins face critical water crises driven by climate volatility.

🌊

70% Over-Irrigation

Traditional continuous flooding wastes millions of liters of reservoir water that could sustain second-season crops.

🌧️

Pumping Before Rain

Lack of localized predictive rainfall watchdogs causes farmers to run pumps hours before massive rainstorms arrive.

📉

Electricity & Fuel Drain

Rural electric grids and diesel pumps run unthrottled for 100+ hours without calculating true thermodynamic crop thirst.

Our Solution: Replace guessing with NASA Earth Observation Data to compute exact liters needed and trigger automated 48-hour rain lockouts.

Satellite Integration

NASA Earth Observations Ingested

OrbitDrop hooks directly into the NASA POWER Agroclimatology API for millimeter-accurate telemetry.

Satellite Parameters (Point Centroid)

  • ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN: Solar Insolation (MJ/m²/day) to calculate solar evaporation drive.
  • T2M / T2M_MIN / T2M_MAX: 2m Surface Air Temperature (°C).
  • RH2M: 2m Relative Humidity (%) for vapor pressure deficit.
  • WS2M: Wind Speed at 2m height for atmospheric aerodynamic resistance.
  • PRECTOTCORR: Calibrated Precipitation (mm) for real-time 48h rain suspension.
FAO-56 Penman-Monteith Core
ETc = ET0 × Kc

Thermodynamic daily thirst scaled by crop phenology (e.g. Paddy Flowering Kc = 1.20).

Dynamic Field Centroid Fetch

Whenever a farmer draws a parcel on the map, Turf.js calculates its exact GPS centroid and calls NASA APIs for that precise basin.

ML & Spatial Agronomy

30-Meter Micro-Stress Downscaling

Moving beyond coarse regional data: Generating actionable 30m root-zone moisture maps.

● Critical Stress (<16%)

Zone below Permanent Wilting Point. Requires 3× targeted water pulse immediately to prevent plant death.

● Moderate Stress (16-24%)

AWD Safe Drying Zone. Aerobic root growth active. Standard irrigation dosage scheduled.

● Optimal Moisture (>24%)

Well-hydrated root layer. Zero water needed — pumps suspended to preserve soil oxygen and prevent methane release.

Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD)

Proven agro-technique from the Sri Lanka Department of Agriculture that slashes paddy water use by 35% without reducing rice yield.

Product Architecture

Real-Time Irrigation Dispatch Engine

A mission-control style web application designed for clarity, instant responsiveness, and field operations.

Interactive Field Mapping

  • Geoman.io Free + Turf.js: Click-to-draw polygon boundaries with automatic geodesic area (ha/ac) and perimeter computation.
  • Auto-Reset on Location Switch: Selecting a new cascade basin flies the map and cleanly resets calculations for a fresh field drawing.
  • Esri World Imagery: High-resolution satellite basemap overlay with sub-meter field boundary visibility.

Smart Dispatch Controls

  • Continuous Water Pump Simulator: Live ticking timer, accumulating liters, animated map spray ripples, and flow rates (LPM / GPM).
  • Delivery Hardware Efficiencies: Precision Drip (90%), Overhead Sprinkler (75%), Furrow (60%), Flood/Basin (50%).
  • Live Agronomic Export Plan: 1-click printable PDF report with NASA observations, reservoir water saved, and CO₂ benefit.
